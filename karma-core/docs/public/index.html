What we do

A trust-minimized
payment layer for AI agents

When AI agents start buying compute, calling services, and collaborating autonomously, they need a payment channel free of trusted third parties. Karma lets agents lock → bind → settle → finalize from their own wallet — fully verifiable, disputable, and circuit-breakable.

Security first

Non-custodial · wallet-only · never touches your keys

Karma never touches, requests, or stores your private keys or seed phrases. Connecting your wallet only performs a one-time EIP-712 signature; your keys stay in your own wallet.

🔑

Self-Custody

Private keys and seed phrases live only in your wallet (MetaMask, etc.). The protocol only verifies signatures — it can never reach your keys.

KEYS NEVER LEAVE WALLET
🛡️

How it works

One trade, four steps

01 · lock

Lock

Lock USDC and mint Bill Tokens 1:1 — funds enter escrow.

→
02 · bind

Bind

Bind buyer and seller bills into an active commitment and begin delivery.

→
03 · settle

Settle

Submit proof of delivery and open the dispute window.

→
04 · finalize

Finalize

No dispute within the window — burn Bills, release funds, done.

On disagreement, open a dispute within the window for verifiable arbitration; on anomalies, CircuitBreaker / EmergencyFreeze can freeze the protocol to protect both parties' funds.
